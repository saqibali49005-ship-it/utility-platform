Common aspect ratios

  • 16:9 — widescreen video and modern displays
  • 9:16 — vertical video
  • 1:1 — square images
  • 4:3 — classic display and photo formats
  • 3:2 — common photography format

How it works

The calculator finds the greatest common divisor of the width and height, then divides both values by it to produce the simplest whole-number ratio.

Good for

  • Image and thumbnail sizing
  • Video export dimensions
  • Website and responsive design
  • Social media graphics

Frequently asked questions

Method & notes

Ratio calculations use positive numeric dimensions and integer greatest-common-divisor reduction. Missing dimensions are calculated from proportional multiplication and rounded to the nearest whole pixel. The tool does not inspect or upload image files.
