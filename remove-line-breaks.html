When to use a line break remover

  • Fix text copied from PDFs where each visual line became a hard return.
  • Unwrap paragraphs copied from emails, webpages and documents.
  • Turn multiline values into one line for forms or single-line fields.
  • Replace line breaks with commas, semicolons, spaces or a custom separator.
