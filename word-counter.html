What is counted?

  • Words: groups of non-whitespace text.
  • Characters: every character including spaces and punctuation.
  • Characters without spaces: excludes spaces and tabs.
  • Sentences: estimated from sentence-ending punctuation.
  • Paragraphs: blocks separated by blank lines.
  • Reading and speaking time: estimates based on standard words-per-minute settings.

Counts are practical estimates and can differ from the rules used by a particular platform, editor or word processor.
