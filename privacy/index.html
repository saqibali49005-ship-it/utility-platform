Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Calculator inputs

The current calculator and utility pages are designed to perform their core processing in your browser. ZemTools does not intentionally send the values you enter into these tools to a ZemTools application server. Some tools can create copied or downloaded results locally in your browser.

Hosting and technical data

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information such as requests, IP addresses, browser information, and security-related logs according to its own policies.

Analytics

ZemTools uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4), a web analytics service provided by Google, to understand how visitors use the website and improve its tools and content. GA4 may collect information such as pages viewed, interactions, approximate location, device and browser details, and online identifiers through cookies or similar technologies. Google may process this information on our behalf. We do not intentionally send the values entered into calculator fields as analytics events. For more information about how Google uses data, see Google's explanation of partner-site data. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Advertising

Advertising is not currently enabled on ZemTools. If advertising services are added, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant data practices.

External links

Links to external websites, including GitHub, are governed by the privacy practices of those websites.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the platform's features and services change. The latest version will be published on this page.
