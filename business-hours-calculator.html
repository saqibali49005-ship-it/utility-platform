What this calculator does

It counts only the working minutes that fall inside your configured weekly schedule, then removes any holiday dates you supplied. The same calendar powers the deadline mode.

Useful for

  • SLA and support response windows
  • Project and contractor working time
  • Billable or chargeable hours
  • Business deadlines and turnaround times

Example

Mon 09:00 → Fri 17:00 = 40 business hours
Fri 15:00 + 10 business hours = Mon 17:00

Method & calculation notes

This tool works in calendar minutes. It does not assume a standard Monday–Friday schedule unless you leave the default schedule unchanged. A business minute is counted only when it falls inside an enabled work period on a non-holiday date.

For general clock-time arithmetic without work schedules, use the Time Calculator.
