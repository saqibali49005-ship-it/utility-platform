What Are Significant Figures?

Significant figures are the digits in a number that carry meaningful information about its precision.

Leading zeros are not significant. Zeros between non-zero digits are significant. Trailing zeros can be significant when the notation makes the intended precision clear.

Examples

  • 0.004560 → 4 significant figures
  • 1002 → 4 significant figures
  • 100.0 → 4 significant figures
  • 1234 → 3 SF → 1230
  • 0.004567 → 2 SF → 0.0046
  • 12.11 + 18.0 → 30.1
