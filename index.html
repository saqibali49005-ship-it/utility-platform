How it works

Significant figures show the meaningful precision of a number. Leading zeros are not significant, while zeros between significant digits are significant. Trailing zeros can depend on notation.

0.00400 → 3 significant figures
1002 → 4 significant figures
1.20e3 → 3 significant figures
