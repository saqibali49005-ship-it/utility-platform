What this calculator does

It finds calendar age between a birth date and a target date, then reports total elapsed calendar days and weeks plus the next birthday date.

How age is calculated

The calculation first finds complete years, then complete months, then the remaining days between the birth-date anniversary and the target date.

Age = complete years + complete months + remaining days

Why dates can differ by a day

Months have different lengths and leap years add an extra February day. Calendar-age results therefore depend on the actual dates rather than an average month length.
